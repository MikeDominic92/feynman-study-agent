# Sanitized Radiation Protection Packet

This file is handmade demo content. It is not copied from private course files.

## ALARA

Use only the amount of radiation needed to answer the clinical question.

## Time, Distance, Shielding

Shorter exposure time, more distance from the source, and useful barriers reduce
exposure.

## Dose Quantities

Air kerma describes energy transferred from the beam to air. Absorbed dose
describes energy kept by tissue. Equivalent dose adjusts for radiation type.
Effective dose also accounts for tissue sensitivity. Dose area product combines
air kerma with exposed field area.
